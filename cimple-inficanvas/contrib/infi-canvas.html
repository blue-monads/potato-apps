Research Canvas
Move: drag a card by its header.
Pan: hold Space and drag, or drag empty canvas.
Zoom: mouse wheel / trackpad or the controls at bottom-right.
Connect: click Connect, then click a card's left/right handle.
Edit: right-click a card or double-click its body.
Delete: select a card and press Delete / Backspace.
Persist: everything is stored in this browser's localStorage.
